About Keybara

Keybara is a modern, fast, clean typing game designed to make typing practice fun instead of boring. It’s built to feel like a mix of a video game, a focus tool, and a customizable learning sandbox.

You get zero clutter, zero confusion, zero friction—just pure typing flow. Themes, word sets, and modes let you play the way you want so “practice” feels like play.

Typing is how your ideas leave your head and show up on the screens that power our world. Improve your typing and everything you do—writing, ideating, coding, messaging, researching—gets faster, cleaner, and easier.

Microsoft’s 2023 Work Trend Index found that the average employee spends 57% of their time communicating in meetings, email, and chat.

A McKinsey analysis cited by HBR shows professionals spend about 30% of the workday on email.

The average adult typing speed sits around 40 WPM. Advanced typists can hit 200+ WPM. For every 10,000 words you type, going from 40 to 80 WPM saves roughly two hours.

Skilled typing frees your cognition so your brain stays on ideas, not the keyboard.

Keybara is built to make typing improvement addictive through:

  • Immersion, clean aesthetics, and zero clutter.
  • Your world, your rules—themes, word sets, and modes.
  • Visible progress: WPM, accuracy, speed graph, and exact typed output vs. target.

Play the game at https://keybara.io.

The Keybara Story

As a kid, video games were my escape from the stressful and confusing real world into one where progress was simple—climbing the Rocket League ranks, filling the Pokédex, or maxing my base in Clash of Clans.

Growing up didn’t kill that itch; it just made indulging in it feel irresponsible. The same long video game sessions that once felt like freedom started to feel like procrastination. But the craving remained: I still wanted that deep, all-absorbing zen state from my favorite childhood video games—while moving my real life forward.

So I asked a question: what if there was something that felt like your favorite video games but secretly leveled up a high-leverage career skill?

I started researching, testing, and building. After late-night prototyping and playtesting, I resolved to build an immersive, simple, fun, customizable typing game.

I didn’t want another typing game that felt like homework. I wanted an expansive sandbox world that pulls you in—a digital realm where you can zone in on a single goal, forget the chaos of the real world, and still walk away tangibly better at something that fuels modern work: typing.

Typing is how your ideas leave your head and show up on the screens that power our modern world. Improve your typing and everything else gets faster, cleaner, and easier.

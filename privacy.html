Keybara.io offers a typing game experience. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and how you can contact us with questions.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate and improve the site.

a. Automatically Collected Data

When you visit Keybara.io we may automatically receive:

  • IP address

  • Operating system

This information is collected using Google Analytics and cookies.

b. Typing Game Data

While using the game, we may collect:

  • Words per minute (WPM)

This data is shown back to you and used to improve gameplay. We do not associate it with your identity.

Keybara.io does not require you to create an account. We do not collect names, emails, or passwords for gameplay.
